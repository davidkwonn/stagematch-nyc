# StageMatch NYC

I want to make a website that connects small artists with venues/bars/music places. the idea is that artists submit themselves into the website with their name, birthday, background, music type, and a video of them playing music for around 30 seconds. The artists want to be paid. 

The backend should focus on storing the information of that artist.

On the other end, we want venues/bars/any type of venues that look for artists to play at their place. They would put their location, type of music they want, name, contact information such as number and email. Once signed up, it should be set up that the artists can take a listen/peek at some artists that are their style by swiping left or right on their videos. If they have a person they want, they can choose to "claim" them.

once claimed, an ai agent will connect the 2 acting as a manager, setting up a date to meet on an online call through google meets. 

there will be an ai agent in the call and once it is confirmed that they are going to perform at the bar/venue/music place, then the venue will pay. 

The artist will receive payment once the venue confirms that they performed at the venue at the end or else refunded. 

The location will be NYC.
